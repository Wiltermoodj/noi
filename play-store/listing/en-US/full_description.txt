THREE REGIONAL DIALECTS SIDE BY SIDE
Vietnamese varies dramatically from North to South. Switch effortlessly between:
• Northern (Hanoi) — crisp 6-tone clarity, "nâu đá", "nhé", "thế"
• Central (Da Nang & Hue) — authentic local words like "ni", "rứa", "chi", "nỏ"
• Southern (Ho Chi Minh City / Saigon) — everyday "nha", "hông", "mắc", "quẹo"

Every curated phrase shows the natural street wording for all three regions, an easy-to-read phonetic guide, the formal textbook equivalent, and a concise street note explaining when and how locals use it.
