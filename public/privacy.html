Nói — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Package ID: app.noi.vietnamese

Nói (“Spoken Vietnamese”) is an offline-first Vietnamese phrasebook, spaced-repetition study deck, pronunciation tone coach, and bidirectional English–Vietnamese translator. We minimize data collection and keep your learning progress private on your device.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your study progress, spaced-repetition intervals, phrase priorities, starred words, custom phrases, dialect preferences, and downloaded offline audio clips are stored solely on your device using local storage and Cache Storage. Nói does not require account registration and does not sync or upload your personal phrasebook or study history to our servers.

2. Microphone & Audio Permissions

Nói requests Android microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) only when you tap Tap to Speak inside Pronunciation Practice:

  • Offline Tone Coach: Microphone audio is analyzed locally on your device to display pitch contours and score Vietnamese tones. Audio clips remain in temporary memory for immediate self-playback and are never transmitted off your device.
  • Optional Speech Recognition: If you enable speech-to-text evaluation, audio is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition service (such as Android Speech Services by Google) under your device's system settings.

3. Optional Regional Vietnamese Text-to-Speech

When Regional Vietnamese Voice is enabled and an audio clip is not yet cached on your device, Nói transmits only the phrase text, selected dialect region (Northern, Central, or Southern), playback speed flag, and speaker voice preference over HTTPS to the Nói TTS service to generate spoken audio. Synthesized audio is cached locally for offline replay. No user identifiers, device IDs, or personal profile fields are attached to TTS requests. You may switch to On-Device Vietnamese Only in Settings at any time to disable all TTS network requests.

4. Third-Party Advertising, Analytics & Tracking

Nói contains no advertisements, no advertising SDKs, no Google Advertising ID collection, and no third-party behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking.

5. Children's Privacy

Nói does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because no accounts or personal identifiers are collected, all study records remain solely on the user's device.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Nói stores your study data locally on your device, you have full control over retention and deletion at any time:

  • Reset your custom phrases and study deck directly inside the app via Settings → Reset phrase deck & custom phrases.
  • Clear all locally stored preferences and cached audio clips via Android Settings → Apps → Nói → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Nói's data practices, please contact the developer at wiltermoodj@gmail.com.
